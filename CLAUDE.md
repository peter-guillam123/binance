Kind: Work

Voice: Guardian house style
Design: its own look (no Guardian fonts, colours, logo or masthead)

landscape, built on the Guardian's reporting plus outside research.

- Publishing: public. Chris said to publish it live on 30 October 2026. It
  deploys to GitHub Pages from main (peter-guillam123.github.io/binance) and
  has a card on the peter-guillam123.github.io index.
- Every figure needs a source in the page's sources list. Flag anything
  uncertain. Never invent quotes or numbers.
- No stock or agency photos. Images are original SVG drawings or clearly
  licensed public-domain material, credited on the page.
